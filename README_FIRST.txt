JARVIS INTERNET SEARCH + VOICE — READY PACKAGE
1. Keep a backup of your current jarvis_web.py and JARVIS_Futuristic_Website.html.
2. Extract this ZIP into a NEW folder in Downloads.
3. Confirm the three main files are together: jarvis_web.py, JARVIS_Futuristic_Website.html, Start_JARVIS.bat.
4. Double-click Start_JARVIS.bat. Keep its black window open.
5. Open http://127.0.0.1:5000 in Chrome.
6. Use INDIA NEWS for live Google News RSS headlines and article links.
7. Click SPEAK and allow microphone access to ask by voice. Replies are spoken by the browser.
8. Uses Ollama model qwen2.5:3b. Requires Ollama to be running and internet for news.
This is a local prototype. News results can vary; check the links. Do not delete your previous files.
